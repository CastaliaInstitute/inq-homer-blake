## Prompt

Create an original narrative illustration for Homer's *Odyssey*, Book 23, the
recognition scene. Penelope and Odysseus face one another in their chamber
after the long trial; the living olive-root bed is the central physical sign
joining house, marriage, and Ithaca. Show disbelief giving way to quiet,
earned tenderness.

Use an original visionary relief-print and hand-colored engraving language:
elongated expressive anatomy, strong contour, disciplined crosshatching,
paper-colored ground, restrained indigo, umber, and muted rose accents. The
image may be Blake-informed in symbolic compression and visionary line, but it
must be wholly original and must not imitate, reproduce, or transform any
particular William Blake plate. Set the scene in a dim Ithacan chamber with
one small oil lamp, moonlit darkness beyond the threshold, and a bed built
around a living olive trunk. Keep the portrait composition trim-safe and
readable at comic-size book scale, with no text.
