## Decisions

- Agamemnon's purification order and the Apollo sacrifice are retained as
  public ritual action, not treated as background between speeches.
- The heralds are not made agents of the seizure: Achilles explicitly says
  Agamemnon is responsible, and Patroclus leads Briseis out.
- “Witnesses before the blessed gods, before mortal men, and before the harsh
  king” preserves Achilles's effort to turn the seizure into a publicly
  remembered charge.
- The appeal to Thetis retains its narrative evidence: the sacking of Thebes,
  the ransom, Apollo's plague, Calchas's prophecy, and Agamemnon's fulfilled
  threat all precede the request for divine intervention.
- The Briareus/Aegaeon episode remains concrete and properly attributed: Thetis
  freed Zeus from the other gods' intended binding by calling the Hundred-Handed
  One to Olympus.
- Thetis's instruction to hold the wrath and withdraw from war is retained,
  along with the precise twelve-day delay caused by Zeus's Ethiopian feast.

This is a working collation, not a final approval record. Apparatus consultation
and a second reader's sign-off are still required before the book can advance.
