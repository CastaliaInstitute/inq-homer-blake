# Contributing

## Image provenance

Every historical image must have a companion record in the relevant manifest.
Include creator, work title, date, collection or source URL, access date, and
scan or reproduction restrictions. Public-domain status of the underlying
work does not automatically make every modern scan unrestricted.

## Generated plates

Store the prompt, model/version, generation date, reference-image identifiers,
curation notes, and post-processing steps. Filenames must distinguish new
generated work from historical Blake plates.

## Text

Do not silently modernize a translation's spelling or punctuation. Any
normalization must be reproducible and recorded in source metadata.
