## Prompt

Create an original narrative illustration for Odyssey Book 23, lines 1–372:
Penelope recognizes Odysseus beside the rooted olive-wood bed in their chamber
after the long trial, with the living root and the house making one quiet
symbolic structure. Use an original visionary relief-print and hand-colored
engraving language: elongated expressive anatomy, strong contour, disciplined
crosshatching, paper-colored ground, restrained indigo, umber, and muted rose
accents. Show Penelope and Odysseus facing one another as disbelief gives way to
tenderness; keep the scene intimate and free of melodrama. Use a single lamp,
moonlit darkness beyond the threshold, and a bed built around a living olive
trunk. Make the portrait composition trim-safe and readable at book scale, with
no text.
