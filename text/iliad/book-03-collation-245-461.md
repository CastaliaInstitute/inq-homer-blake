## Decisions recorded

- **245–266 — Priam’s summons:** Restored the heralds’ lambs, wine, leather flask, mixing bowl, golden cups, Priam’s fearful departure, Antenor’s place in the chariot, and their passage through the Scaean Gates.
- **267–302 — Oath ritual:** Restored Agamemnon and Odysseus rising, water poured over the kings’ hands, hairs cut from the lambs, the witnesses of Zeus, Sun, rivers, earth, and the underworld, the conditional settlements, and the curse upon oath-breakers.
- **303–323 — Priam’s withdrawal:** Preserved Priam’s refusal to watch his son fight, his uncertainty about the fated death, the return to Ilium, and the communal prayer for the guilty party to enter Hades.
- **324–345 — Lot and armor:** Restored Hector’s backward glance, Paris’s first lot, the exact armor sequence, and the amazement and measured space between the champions.
- **346–378 — Weapons and helmet:** Restored Paris’s bent spear, Menelaus’s prayer concerning guest-friendship and wrongdoing, the spear through shield/corselet/tunic, the broken sword, the horsehair grip, the choking strap, and Aphrodite’s breaking of the strap.
- **379–382 — Divine rescue:** Preserved Aphrodite’s immediate snatching of Paris, dense mist, and placement in the fragrant incense-filled chamber.
- **383–427 — Aphrodite and Helen:** Restored the goddess’s aged spinner disguise, Helen’s recognition, the threat of renewed hatred and death, Helen’s silent departure under divine guidance, and the attendants’ return to work.
- **428–447 — Helen and Paris:** Preserved Helen’s wish that Paris had died, her challenge to him to fight again, Paris’s appeal to Athena and the gods, Cranae, and the renewed erotic longing.
- **448–461 — Public aftermath:** Restored the perforated bed frame, Menelaus’s unanswered search, the universal hatred of Paris among the allies, and Agamemnon’s public declaration that Menelaus has won and that Helen and the possessions must be returned.
