## Prompt

in the guise of a guest, standing at the threshold of Odysseus's household

off-center, deep interior receding behind her, quiet margins, no text. Clear
sea light outside against a darker hall, alert stillness, hospitality carrying
hidden danger. Black, warm paper white, restrained gray wash, engraved
hatching, handmade paper grain.
