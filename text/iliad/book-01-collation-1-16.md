## Decisions

- **“wrath” for *mēnis*:** retains the poem's marked, superhuman register;
  “anger” was too weak for the first word's narrative force.
- **“stood at odds”:** renders the reciprocal separation in *diastēten*
  without adding a psychological explanation that the Greek does not supply.
- **“joined them in strife”:** preserves the question's causative ambiguity;
  Apollo is named in the answer, while the English does not imply that he
  intended the entire quarrel.
- **“plague”:** makes *nosos* concrete and keeps the causal sequence visible.
- **“ransom,” “wreath,” and “golden staff”:** restores the objects in 1.13–15;
  the earlier draft compressed them into the later description of Chryses'
  prayer.

This is a working collation, not a final approval record. Apparatus consultation
and a second reader's sign-off are still required before the book can advance.
