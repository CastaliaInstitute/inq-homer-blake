# Odyssey Book 7 — read-aloud and fidelity review

**Review status:** translator self-review; independent reader still required

## Findings

The prior manuscript was materially wrong in shape: it began with Athena's
dream to Nausicaa and then continued through the games and Demodocus, importing
Book 8 material. The revised manuscript now follows the collated Book 7 arc:
Nausicaa's return, Athena's escort, the palace catalogue, Odysseus's hearth
supplication, Arete's clothing recognition, the Calypso chronology, and the bed
prepared beneath the portico.

The revision retains the fifty serving women, two springs, successive fruit,
Hephaestus's deathless dogs, Hermes's final cup, and Arete's authority. Odysseus's
chronology is explicit: nine days on the keel, seven years with Calypso, seventeen
days at sea, and the mountains on the eighteenth day.

The palace catalogue benefits from long, accumulating sentences and recurring
gold-and-silver imagery in a Longfellow-like narrative register. The hearth scene
turns through shorter imperatives and bodily hunger toward human urgency. The
repeated syntax of the final speech remains a line-level meter risk.

## Open decisions

- Confirm the condensed genealogy against the full Greek before publication.
- Check the belly passage and all line breaks in the two-column proof.
- Commission an independent read-aloud and a second-reader omissions/additions pass.

**Conclusion:** structurally corrected and substantially more faithful; not yet
approved for layout or final publication.
