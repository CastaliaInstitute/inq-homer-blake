## Decisions

- “Wrath of Apollo” restores the Greek *mēnis* and keeps the divine register
  audible; “far-shooting bow” carries the force of the accompanying epithet.
- “Ready in word and hand” makes Calchas's request for verbal and physical
  protection explicit without turning the line into prose explanation.
- “Savor of lambs / and goats made perfect in sacrifice” preserves the material
  ritual image in Achilles's question and the animal specificity of the copy
  text.
- The English retains the political progression: Calchas fears the king,
  Achilles guarantees protection, and Calchas identifies the dishonored priest
  before Agamemnon demands a replacement prize.
- “Replacement prize” remains distinct from “honor”; the material *geras*
  embodies the social *timē* without making the two Greek terms interchangeable.

This is a working collation, not a final approval record. Apparatus consultation
