## Prompt

Create a high-resolution portrait illustration of Athena arriving at the
threshold of Odysseus's household in Homer’s *Odyssey* Book 1, an original work
for CastaliaInstitute. Show the stone court and great doorway of an Ithacan
palace at evening, proud suitors deeper inside, and the sea and hills beyond.
Athena disguised as Mentes is a poised traveler with a long spear; young
Telemachus receives her. Suggest the divine identity through posture and light,
not modern fantasy effects. Use original visionary black ink and restrained
color, etched contour, compressed anatomy, symbolic intensity, handmade line
variation, and Blake-like visionary energy without copying any specific Blake
plate. Use a tall portrait with clear threshold geometry, quiet margins, dusk
light, parchment, smoke gray, muted sea blue, antique gold, paper grain, and
controlled wash. No text, watermark, signature, modern objects, fantasy armor,
or visible typography.
