## Scene brief

Odysseus clings to the surviving timbers of his broken raft as Poseidon’s storm
drives him toward the Phaeacian shore. The human figure should remain small
against the elemental field: exhausted, intelligent endurance rather than
adventure spectacle. A distant coast may barely emerge through the rain, while
the broken bindings and foam keep the scene materially grounded.
