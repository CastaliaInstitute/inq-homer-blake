## Prompt

Create an original narrative illustration for *Odyssey* Book 13, lines 1–440:
Odysseus newly landed at Ithaca as Athena reveals the harbor, olive tree, and
cave of the nymphs before disguising him for the next stage of his return. Show
a veiled Phorcys harbor in dawn-dark light: Odysseus low and vulnerable near the
shore, Athena centered in a shepherd’s plain guise, her divine authority implied
by posture and light rather than fantasy effects. Let the olive tree and cave
form a stable rooted axis while the sea recedes into a narrow opening behind
them. Use original etched black contour, relief-print pressure, selective
crosshatching, warm paper white, smoke gray, muted sea-indigo, and a restrained
olive-gold wash. Keep the portrait composition trim-safe and readable at comic
book scale, with quiet negative space for caption and folio and no text.
