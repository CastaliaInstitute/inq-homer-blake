# *Iliad*, Book 5, lines 1–165 — collation record

**Method:** The English was checked against the locked Greek XML by numbered line range. Athena’s fire/star image, the Dares episode, the opening sequence of named deaths, Pandarus’s wound, the prayer, and Diomedes’ first aristeia were checked individually.

## Decisions recorded

- **1–26 — Athena and Dares:** Restored the fire of the autumn star, Dares as Hephaestus’ priest, Phegeus’ missed spear, Idaios’ flight, Hephaestus’ concealment, and the horses sent to the ships.
- **27–83 — First named deaths:** Restored Athena’s withdrawal of Ares, Odius, Phaestus, Scamandrius and Artemis, Phereclus and his ships, Pedaeus and Theano, and Hypsenor and the severed arm.
- **84–94 — Diomedes’ torrent:** Preserved the uncertainty whether Diomedes belongs to Trojans or Achaeans and the bridge-and-orchard-fence simile.
- **95–113 — Pandarus’ arrow:** Restored the right shoulder wound, Pandarus’s boast, Diomedes’ withdrawal before the horses, Sthenelus’s extraction of the arrow, and the blood through the twisted tunic.
- **114–143 — Athena’s commission:** Restored Diomedes’ prayer, the inherited courage of Tydeus, the removal of the mist, recognition of god and man, and the injunction to wound Aphrodite but not fight the other gods.
- **144–165 — Diomedes’ first surge:** Restored the wounded-lion simile, Astynous and Hypeiron, Abas and Polyidus, Xanthus and Thoon, the grief of Phaenops, and Echemmon and Chromius with the lion-and-cattle image.
