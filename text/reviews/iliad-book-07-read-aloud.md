# Iliad Book 7 — read-aloud and fidelity revision log

**Scope:** Iliad 7.1–482, current working translation  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass  
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This pass tests the Hector–Ajax duel, the exchange of gifts, the corpse truce,
and the wall episode for audible momentum, ceremonial register, and fidelity-
sensitive details. It does not certify meter, establish a final Greek text, or
replace an independent omissions/additions review.

## Critical notes

- Beauty is strongest where the poem lets public ritual carry emotion: the silent
  armies, the lot, the gifts, and the paired pyres. Keep explanatory glosses out
  of those transitions.
- The draft’s Longfellow register should remain spacious and grave, but the duel
  needs harder consonants and shorter clauses so that the prose-like blank verse
  does not flatten impact.
- The machine meter report is only a syllable screen. A named human reader must
  still review stress, proper names, repeated epithets, and intentional outliers.
