**Scope:** Iliad 13.1–837, current working translation

**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass

## Critical notes

- Beauty comes from the tension between fixed places and fluid forces: ship
  benches, sterns, gates, and shield rims hold against Poseidon’s sea-like motion
  and the storm imagery that repeatedly threatens to dissolve the line.
- The Longfellow-inspired register should keep Idomeneus’s speeches bright with
  heroic wit while allowing the wounded and displaced to remain fully human; do
  not let the catalogue of names erase the cost of each encounter.
- The meter report is only a syllable screen. A named human reader must still
  review stress, proper names, disguise transitions, spatial orientation, and the
  long chain of paired combats.
