## Decisions

- The Chryse episode is restored as a complete ritual sequence rather than a
  narrative shortcut: return, prayer, sacrifice, cooking, shared portions,
  paean, night, wind, and return all remain visible.
- The twelfth-day transition is retained. It links Thetis's promise to the
  actual arrival of the gods on Olympus and prevents the request from appearing
  to occur at an unspecified dawn.
- “Incline my head” and “cannot be revoked” preserve the physical and legal
  force of Zeus's nod; it is an act of divine confirmation, not a casual assent.
- Hera's suspicion and Zeus's threat remain a genuine domestic power struggle;
  the passage is not modernized into comic banter before Hephaestus mediates.
- Hephaestus's fall and Lemnian rescue remain part of his intervention, while
  the closing feast retains the poem's movement from conflict to order, song,
  and sleep.

This is a working collation, not a final approval record. Apparatus consultation
and a second reader's sign-off are still required before the book can advance.
