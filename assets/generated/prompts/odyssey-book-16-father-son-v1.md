## Prompt

Create an original narrative illustration for Odyssey Book 16, lines 1–481:
Telemachus recognizes the disguised Odysseus in the humble swineherd’s hut, with
Athena’s unseen presence suggested through light and threshold rather than a
literal deity portrait. Show the instant father and son move from astonishment
to embrace; Odysseus remains marked by disguise, and Telemachus is young and
travel-worn. Use a rough Ithacan hut, low hearth, hides, olive trees, and an open
doorway toward the hills. Render it in an original visionary relief-print and
hand-colored engraving language: strong contour, disciplined crosshatching,
paper-colored ground, restrained indigo, umber, and muted gold. Make the
portrait composition trim-safe and readable at book scale, with no text.
