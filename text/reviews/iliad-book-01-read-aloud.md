## Critical notes

- The current lineation has a strong formal register and generally avoids
  contemporary idiom; “wrath,” “honor,” “far-shooting,” and “swift ships” form
  a coherent elevated lexicon in the Longfellow-inspired mode.
- Several lines use long syntactic runs deliberately. They should be tested at
  the page's actual two-column measure before any further shortening, because
  a line that sounds stately aloud may become visually dense in print.
- The repeated Agamemnon passage at the boundary of the former 68–100 and
  101–430 sections was removed on 2026-09-03. The assembled text export and
  source-coverage preflight were regenerated afterward.
- “Working range” labels in the manuscript remain editorial cautions; they are
  not evidence that the English has passed a line-level comparison.
