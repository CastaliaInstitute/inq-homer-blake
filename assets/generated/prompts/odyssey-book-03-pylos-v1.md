## Prompt

Create a new monochrome visionary illustration for Homer’s *Odyssey*, Book 3,
showing Telemachus arriving at Nestor’s shore sacrifice at Pylos. Set the scene
on a broad Mediterranean shoreline at dawn, with the dark sea behind a ritual
gathering of Pylian men and black bulls. Telemachus stands modestly at the
threshold of hospitality while Nestor’s household receives him; ritual smoke,
horses, and shore form clear symbolic bands. Use original engraved-looking line
with visionary severity, rhythmic contour, elongated but anatomically grounded
gesture, and Blake-informed intensity without imitating or reproducing any
specific William Blake plate. Portrait composition, strong vertical hierarchy,
quiet outer margin for comic-size trim and caption, readable at thumbnail scale.
Use black, warm paper, and restrained gray wash only, with fine etched line,
stone, wool, smoke, sea, and worked bronze. No text, border, watermark,
publisher marks, modern objects, fantasy armor, or gore. This is an original
CastaliaInstitute concept and not a William Blake work.
