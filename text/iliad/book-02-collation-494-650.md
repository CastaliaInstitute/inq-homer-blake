# *Iliad*, Book 2, lines 494–650 — collation record

**Source lock:** `data/tlg0012/tlg001/tlg0012.tlg001.perseus-grc2.xml`

**Source revision:** PerseusDL `canonical-greekLit` commit `ac0bc60033f1f83990a5cf7f1e7fc2e0423e381a`

**Method:** The English was checked against the locked Greek XML by numbered line range, with particular attention to proper names, leadership, genealogies, contingent boundaries, and ship counts. This is an editorial working record, not a claim of a fully established critical text.

## Decisions recorded

- **494–510 — Boeotians:** Restored the five named leaders—Peneleus, Leitus, Arcesilaus, Prothoenor, and Clonius—and the full settlement list through Anthedon. Retained fifty ships and one hundred twenty men per ship.
- **511–516 — Aspledon and Orchomenos:** Restored Ascalaphus and Ialmenus, their descent from Ares through Astyoche, and the secret encounter that explains their birth; retained thirty ships.
- **517–526 — Phocians:** Restored Schedius and Epistrophus, their genealogy, the full place list, forty ships, and the Phocian formation on the Boeotian left.
- **527–535 — Locrians:** Preserved the lesser Ajax’s comparison with Telamonian Ajax, his slight build and light armor, his excellence with the spear, the complete place list, forty ships, and the location beyond Euboea.
- **536–545 — Abantes:** Restored the complete Euboean place list, Elephenor’s genealogy, the Abantes’ hair and ashwood spears, and forty ships.
- **546–558 — Athenians and Salamis:** Restored the Erechtheus/Athena foundation story, Menestheus’s comparison with Nestor, fifty Athenian ships, and Ajax’s twelve ships placed beside them.
- **559–568 — Argos and Diomedes:** Restored the complete Argive place list, the three commanders and their genealogies, Diomedes’ overall command, and eighty ships.
- **569–580 — Mycenae and Agamemnon:** Restored the full place list, the Adrastus aside, one hundred ships, the exceptional size and quality of Agamemnon’s contingent, and his conspicuous armor.
- **581–590 — Lacedaemon and Menelaus:** Restored the complete place list, sixty ships, Menelaus’s separate formation, and his motive of avenging Helen’s distress and tears.
- **591–602 — Pylos and Nestor:** Restored Thryon at the Alpheius crossing, Epi, the complete Dorion/Thamyris episode, Nestor’s Gerenian epithet, and ninety ships.
- **603–614 — Arcadia:** Restored the Aepytus tomb and close-combat detail, all Arcadian settlements, Agapenor’s genealogy, sixty ships, and Agamemnon’s provision of vessels because the Arcadians were not seafarers.
- **615–624 — Elis:** Corrected the passage from a forty-ship single command to four leaders and four divisions of ten ships: Amphimachus, Thalpius, Diores, and Polyxenus, with their genealogies.
- **625–630 — Dulichium and the Echinades:** Restored Meges, Phyleus, the reason for Phyleus’s exile from Dulichium, and forty ships.
- **631–637 — Cephallenians:** Corrected the island list and retained Odysseus’s counsel-epithet, mainland opposite, and twelve red-cheeked ships.
- **638–644 — Aetolians:** Restored Thoas’s genealogy, the full place list, the succession note after Oeneus and Meleager, and forty ships.
- **645–650 — Cretans:** Restored Idomeneus’s command and the complete city list through the “hundred-citied” Crete description; the next source block continues the Cretan ships and subsequent contingents.
