## Decisions recorded

- **1–38 — Divine council:** Restored Hebe’s nectar service, Zeus’s provocation, Athena and Hera’s silent hostility, and the choice to test the oath rather than flatten the gods’ competing motives.
- **39–72 — Zeus and Hera:** Restored Zeus’s argument about Ilium’s sacrifices and his reciprocal bargain with Hera, including Hera’s three beloved cities and her instruction to send Athena.
- **73–104 — Athena’s descent and commission:** Restored the star-sign simile, Laodocus’s likeness, Pandarus’s position among Aesepus’s people, the promised Trojan honor, and the Apollo vow.
- **105–126 — Pandarus’s bow:** Restored the wild-goat origin, sixteen-palm horns, horn-worker’s craft, shield cover, unopened arrow, and bow/string sounds.
- **127–147 — Menelaus wounded:** Preserved Athena’s maternal-fly comparison, the belt/corselet/guard sequence, the arrow’s graze, the dark blood, and the Maeonian/Carian ivory-and-purple image.
- **148–170 — Agamemnon and Machaon:** Restored the initial fear, Menelaus’s recovery of courage, Agamemnon’s oath-centered lament and prophecy of Ilium’s fall, the call for Machaon, and the healer’s removal of belt, corselet, guard, clotted blood, and Cheiron’s medicine.
