# Iliad Book 15 — read-aloud and fidelity revision log

**Scope:** Iliad 15.1–746, current working translation  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass  
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This pass tests Zeus’s restored command, the divine withdrawals, Apollo’s
restoration of Hector, the battle at the ships, and the transition that summons
Patroclus. It does not certify meter or replace an independent omissions/additions
review.

## Critical notes

- Beauty depends on the book’s tightening scale: divine command becomes mist,
  mist becomes sight, sight becomes a ship’s stern, and the stern becomes a
  bridge where a single hero gives ground.
- The Longfellow-inspired register should keep the repeated divine formulas grave,
  but the ship-front needs short, physical clauses. Preserve the difference between
  a deck, a bridge, a stern-post, a spear, and a firebrand.
- The meter report is only a syllable screen. A named human reader must still
  review stress, proper names, spatial orientation, and the transition from
  Patroclus’s compassion to Ajax’s last stand.
