# Odyssey Book 16 — read-aloud and fidelity review

**Review status:** translator self-review, revision pass; independent reader still required
**Revision date:** 2026-09-03

## Findings

The recognition sequence is preserved: Telemachus enters as a known son to
Eumaeus but still treats Odysseus as a stranger; Athena appears only to the
father and the dogs; Odysseus is restored, doubted, and then recognized. The
scene therefore makes knowledge itself part of the tactical plan.

The suitor census is kept in its source categories: fifty-two Ithacans, six
servants from Dulichium, twenty-four from Same, twenty from Zacynthus, twelve
additional Ithacans, plus Medon, the singer, and two serving men. The weapon
plan retains its cover story about smoke damage and drunken quarrels, its two-
man armory, its testing of the servants, and the instruction that the secret
remain between father and son until the household is assessed.

## Beauty and cadence

The scene should move from domestic tenderness to compressed military planning.
The bird-of-prey comparison for the father and son’s weeping needs emotional
force without becoming ornamental; the later count of suitors should sound like
an inventory that makes the danger measurable. The return to Eumaeus’s hut at
night restores the book’s quiet register. The ship’s return, the suitors’ plot,
Penelope’s rebuke, and Eurymachus’s false reassurance were expanded into verse
in this pass; the working text still awaits independent Greek-fidelity and
read-aloud review.

## Revision log — 2026-09-03

- **16.262–270, Greek fidelity:** The suitor census now assigns the fifty-two
  suitors and six servants to Dulichium, followed by twenty-four from Same,
  twenty from Zacynthus, and twelve from Ithaca. The earlier draft incorrectly
  attached the fifty-two to Ithaca, changing the political geography and the
  scale of the threat.
- **16.452–458, Greek fidelity:** “A yearling boar” is corrected to “a
  five-year-old boar,” restoring the explicit age of the sacrificial animal.

These are translator revisions, not independent approval. A named reviewer must
still compare the census and closing household sequence against the pinned Greek.

**Conclusion:** recognition and tactical logic are preserved; not approved for
