# Design specification

## Finished format

- Two separate hardcover volumes: *Iliad* and *Odyssey*
- Primary trim: standard US comic size, exactly 6.625 × 10.25 in
- Interior stock: 80# coated paper
- Printing: color, subject to final printer profile and proof
- Bleed: 0.125 in on all trimmed edges when artwork reaches the edge; full-bleed
  document size 6.875 × 10.5 in
- Final cover spread and spine width: calculated from the locked page count
  and current printer specification

The stock target follows Lulu's current [paper-stock documentation](https://help.lulu.com/en/support/solutions/articles/64000255473-cover-and-interior-paper-stocks):
80# White Coated is the image-oriented interior stock, paired here with
Premium Color. The casewrap cover stock is a separate printer specification;
the final spread and spine must be built from Lulu's generated template after
each interior page count is locked.

## Page system

Use a shared typographic system across both volumes: restrained classical
display treatment, source-line-preserving two-column verse, generous outer margins, and a
consistent running architecture for book numbers, notes, captions, and plates.
The final grid, typefaces, point sizes, leading, and color values must be
recorded here before production pages are assembled.

## Image system

Historical Blake images retain their documented titles and credits. New plates
may respond to Blake's contour, symbolism, and visionary compression, but must
be credited as new work and identified in captions and metadata. Do not imitate
or alter a specific Blake plate in a way that obscures its provenance.

## Deliverables

Each volume produces one print-ready interior PDF, one print-ready cover PDF,
an accessible text export where practical, and a packaged manifest containing
all text, image, font, and preflight metadata.
