# Odyssey Book 6 — read-aloud revision log

**Scope:** Odyssey 6.1–331, current working translation
**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This pass checks Athena's dream, Nausicaa's laundry expedition, Odysseus's
supplication, the road to the palace, and the final guest welcome for narrative
poise, social register, and fidelity-sensitive compression. It does not certify
meter or Greek accuracy. Independent source comparison, full stress analysis,
and human sign-off remain open.

## Critical notes

- The chapter's formal movement is from unwashed cloth to clothing, from naked
  exposure to a cloak, and from the shore to a hearth. Material restoration
  should remain audible at every step.
- The translation's elevated register is strongest when it lets hospitality be
  an action rather than an abstract virtue. Avoid slang or modern therapeutic
  language in Odysseus's plea and Nausicaa's counsel.
- The river, Athena, Ino's earlier veil, Calypso's clothing, and Arete's clothing
  recognition form a cross-book vocabulary chain that requires concordance
  review before layout.
