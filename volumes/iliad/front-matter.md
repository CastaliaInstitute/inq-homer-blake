# *Iliad* volume front matter plan

The final volume should include, in this order:

1. Half title
2. Title page and edition statement
3. Copyright, translation, image, and source acknowledgments
4. **Forward by a.thucydides**
5. Translator's note describing the Greek base text and Longfellow formal
  model
6. Contents
7. List of historical and original plates
8. The twenty-four books, with notes and documented plate captions
9. Translation glossary and source notes
10. Colophon with edition specifications and preflight date

The title page must describe the images accurately, for example: “with
historical engravings associated with William Blake and original plates by
Castalia Institute.” It must not imply that Blake created newly generated work.
Do not use that visual-program line in an illustration-free edition or proof.

## Canonical publication credits

- **Title:** *The Iliad*
- **Author:** Homer
- **Translation:** Castalia Institute; translated from the pinned Greek copy
  text in a Longfellow-inspired English verse form
- **Illustration:** credit each included plate by its documented creator role.
  Name John Flaxman as designer and William Blake only as engraver where the
  plate record supports those roles; credit original plates to Castalia
  Institute and state that they are not by William Blake.

No blanket illustrator credit is authorized until the final plate selection
and rights review are complete.
