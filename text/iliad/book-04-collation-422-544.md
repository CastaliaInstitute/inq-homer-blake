## Decisions recorded

- **422–456 — Armies collide:** Restored the wave-on-the-shore simile, silent Achaean command structure, Trojan sheep image, mixed languages, and the personified forces of Ares, Athena, Deimos, Phobos, and Strife.
- **457–470 — Echepolus and Elephenor:** Restored Antilochus’s first kill, the helmet and forehead wound, Elephenor’s attempt to strip the body, and Agenor’s counter-stroke.
- **471–504 — Simoeisius, Leucus, and Democoon:** Restored the wolf image, Simoeisius’s birth and poplar simile, Antiphus’s missed cast, Leucus’s death, Odysseus’s anger, Democoon’s Abydos origin, and the withdrawal before Apollo’s intervention.
- **505–516 — Apollo and Athena:** Preserved Apollo’s speech about mortal flesh and Achilles’ anger, and Athena’s movement among the Achaeans who slacken.
- **517–538 — Diores, Peirous, and Thoas:** Restored the ankle and tendon wound, the Thracian and Aetolian commanders, the lung and belly blows, the unstripped armor, and the paired leaders lying in the dust.
- **539–544 — Closing image:** Preserved the divine protection required to cross the battle unstruck and the final field of Trojans and Achaeans lying face-down together.
