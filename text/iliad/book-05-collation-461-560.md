**Method:** The English was checked against the locked Greek XML by numbered line range. Ares’ disguise and speech, Sarpedon’s rebuke, the threshing-floor and cloud similes, Agamemnon’s exhortation, and the Crethon/Orsilochus genealogy were checked in sequence.

## Decisions recorded

- **461–470 — Ares and Priam’s sons:** Restored Ares in Acamas’s likeness, the appeal to save Aeneas, and the direct command to Priam’s sons.
- **471–493 — Sarpedon’s rebuke:** Preserved Sarpedon’s accusation that Hector has lost his courage, his own Lycian distance and family, his warning about the city’s fate, and his demand for sustained command.
- **494–527 — Renewed ranks:** Restored Hector’s leap from the chariot, the threshing-floor/chaff image, Ares’s darkness, Apollo’s command, and the cloud image for the steadfast Danaans.
- **528–540 — Agamemnon and Deicoon:** Restored Agamemnon’s “be men” exhortation, mutual shame as a survival force, and Deicoon’s wound through shield, belt, and lower belly.
- **541–560 — Aeneas and the sons of Diocles:** Restored Crethon and Orsilochus, their Alpheius genealogy, their reason for joining the expedition, and the lion image of their death.
