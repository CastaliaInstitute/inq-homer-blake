## Prompt

Create an original monochrome relief-like line and wash illustration for
Homer's *Iliad*, Book 11, centered on Patroclus arriving at the tent of the
wounded Eurypylus and carrying the battlefield's crisis toward Achilles. Show
Patroclus bending over Eurypylus to tend the arrow wound, with the wounded hero
recumbent; let a dark suggestion of the ships and a narrow path toward
Achilles' tent recede behind them. Use a vertical plate with a strong central
diagonal from Patroclus's hands toward the distant tent, a quiet outer margin
for caption and trim safety, and a pallid medical light against battlefield
shadow. The mood is compassion under pressure, solemn and luminous. Use black,
warm ivory, and restrained umber-gray wash, with engraved contour, paper grain,
and controlled crosshatching. Blake-informed in atmosphere but not imitating or
reproducing any specific William Blake work. No text, border, publisher mark,
watermark, modern objects, fantasy armor, gore, glossy digital rendering,
cartoon styling, or anachronistic anatomy.
