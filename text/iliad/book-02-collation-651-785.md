# *Iliad*, Book 2, lines 651–785 — collation record

**Source revision:** PerseusDL `canonical-greekLit` commit `ac0bc60033f1f83990a5cf7f1e7fc2e0423e381a`

**Method:** The English was checked against the locked Greek XML by numbered line range. Particular attention was given to contingent succession, named genealogies, absent leaders, ship counts, and the catalogue’s closing ranking of horses and heroes.

## Decisions recorded

- **651–652 — Cretans:** Restored Meriones as Idomeneus’s equal, and retained the eighty-ship total for the Cretan contingent.
- **653–670 — Rhodes:** Restored Tlepolemus’s nine ships, the three Rhodian divisions, his descent from Heracles and Astyoche, the killing of Licymnius, his flight, and Zeus’s favor toward the Rhodians.
- **671–680 — Syme and the Calydnian islands:** Restored Nireus’s three ships and beauty ranking, his small following, the island list, the Heraclid genealogy of Pheidippus and Antiphus, and thirty ships.
- **681–694 — Achilles’ contingent:** Restored Pelasgian Argos, Alos, Alope, Trachis, Phthia, and Hellas; the names Myrmidons/Hellenes/Achaeans; fifty ships; Achilles’ absence; and the Briseis, Lyrnessus, Mynes, and Epistrophus background.
- **695–710 — Protesilaus and Podarces:** Restored the full Phylace place list, Protesilaus’s death as the first Achaean killed while leaving his ship, Podarces’ replacement command, and forty ships.
- **711–715 — Eumelus:** Restored Pherae’s local geography, Alcestis’s genealogy, and eleven ships.
- **716–728 — Philoctetes and Medon:** Restored the four-place contingent, Philoctetes’ seven ships and fifty bowmen per ship, his Lemnos wound and abandonment, and Medon’s illegitimate descent from Oileus and Rhene.
- **729–759 — Thessalian contingents:** Restored the physician sons of Asclepius and thirty ships; Euryphylus and forty; Polypoetes and Leonteus with their Lapith/Caeneid genealogies and forty; Guneus with twenty-two; the Enienes and Perrhaebians and the Titaresius/Styx simile; and Prothous’s Magnetes with forty.
- **760–770 — Catalogue ranking:** Preserved the second Muse invocation, Eumelus’s exceptional mares, Apollo’s role in their breeding, and Telamonian Ajax as best among the men while Achilles remains absent.
- **771–785 — Achilles’ camp and advance:** Restored Achilles’ anger, the idle activities of his people, the horses grazing beside the chariots, the absent leader’s effect on the camp, and the earth-fire/Typhoeus simile as the Achaeans cross the plain.
