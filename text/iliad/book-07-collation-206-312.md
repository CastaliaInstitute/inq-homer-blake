## Decisions recorded

  seven ox-hides plus bronze, and Ajax’s reminder that other champions remain after
  Achilles.
- **233–272 — the duel:** Preserved Hector’s explicit refusal of a secret blow, the
  penetration of six hides, the opposed spear wounds, the two stones, and Apollo’s
  raising of Hector.
- **273–312 — truce and gifts:** Restored Idaeus and Talthybius, the appeal to night,
  the mutual respect in Hector’s speech, the silver-studded sword, purple belt, and
  the two armies’ response.
