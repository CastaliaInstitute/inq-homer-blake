# Iliad Book 9 — read-aloud and fidelity revision log

**Scope:** Iliad 9.1–713, current working translation  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass  
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This pass tests the night council, the embassy to Achilles, the three distinct
voices of Odysseus, Phoenix, and Ajax, and Achilles’ response concerning honor,
life, and the two fates. It does not certify meter or replace an independent
omissions/additions review.

## Findings and actions

| Passage | Finding | Action or decision |
|---|---|---|

| 9.606–665 | Phoenix’s biography must sound like memory and moral pressure, not a detachable digression. | Keep Amyntor, the concubine, the Erinyes, the nine-night watch, Peleus’s welcome, and the Dolopes; recheck family relationships independently. |
| 9.666–713 | The Meleager exemplum and Ajax’s blunt appeal prepare a refusal that is emotionally intelligible, not merely stubborn. | Preserve Calydon, Althaea, Cleopatra, the delayed gifts, Ajax’s murdered-kin analogy, and Achilles’ condition for rejoining the war. |

## Critical notes

- Beauty depends on the embassy’s changing temperature: formal gifts, intimate
  hospitality, hard argument, tears, mythic memory, and blunt friendship. Keep
  each voice audibly distinct.
- The Longfellow-inspired register should let the two-fates speech broaden into
  grave, lucid meditation; avoid smoothing away the disturbing equivalence Achilles
  draws between glory and survival.
- The meter report is only a syllable screen. A named human reader must still
  review stress, proper names, repeated formulas, and the long speeches’ breath.
