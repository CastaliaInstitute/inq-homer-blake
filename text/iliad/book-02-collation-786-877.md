# *Iliad*, Book 2, lines 786–877 — collation record

**Source lock:** `data/tlg0012/tlg001/tlg0012.tlg001.perseus-grc2.xml`

**Source revision:** PerseusDL `canonical-greekLit` commit `ac0bc60033f1f83990a5cf7f1e7fc2e0423e381a`

**Method:** The English was checked against the locked Greek XML by numbered line range. Proper names, command relationships, geographic modifiers, genealogies, and the sequence of the Trojan muster were checked individually.

## Decisions recorded

- **786–801 — Iris at Priam’s gates:** Restored Iris’s arrival from Zeus, her likeness to Polites, the watch from Aesyetes’ tomb, the leaf-and-sand comparison, and the scale of the approaching Achaean force.
- **802–815 — Hector’s order:** Preserved Hector as the addressee, the many allied tongues, the instruction that each commander marshal his own people, the opening gates, and Batieia/Myrine as the point where Trojans and allies separated into formation.
- **816–823 — Trojan and Dardanian command:** Restored Hector’s principal command, Aeneas’s descent from Anchises and Aphrodite, and the supporting command of Antenor’s sons Archelochus and Acamas.
- **824–834 — Zeleia and Adrasteia:** Restored Pandarus’s descent from Lycaon and Apollo’s gift of the bow; restored Adrasteia, Apaesus, Pityeia, and Mount Tereia, with Merops’s failed warning to his sons Adrastus and Amphius.
- **835–839 — Percote through Arisba:** Restored the full place list and Asius’s identity, horses, and origin beside the Selleis.
- **840–850 — Pelasgians, Thracians, Cicones, and Paeonians:** Restored Pyleus beside Hippothous, the full Pelasgian genealogy, the Thracian command of Acamas and Pirous, Euphemus’s Ciconian command, and Pirechmes’s Paeonian force from Amydon and the Axios.
- **851–857 — Paphlagonians and Halizonians:** Restored Pylaemenes, Eneti’s wild asses, the Parthenius and complete Paphlagonian place list, and Odius and Epistrophus from silver-bearing Alybe.
- **858–866 — Mysians, Phrygians, and Maionians:** Restored Chromis and Ennomus, the failure of augury to save Ennomus, Phorcys and Ascanius, and Mesthles and Antiphus beneath Tmolus.
- **867–875 — Carians:** Restored the “barbarous-speaking” descriptor, the full Carian geography, the brothers Amphimachus and Nastes, Nastes’s gold, and Achilles’ later killing and removal of the gold.
- **876–877 — Lycians:** Restored Sarpedon and blameless Glaucus as leaders from Lycia and the whirling Xanthus.
