# Iliad Book 14 — read-aloud and fidelity revision log

**Scope:** Iliad 14.1–522, current working translation  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass  
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This pass tests Nestor’s counsel, the wounded leaders’ debate, Hera’s deception,
Sleep’s bargain, Poseidon’s intervention, and the final ship-front advance. It
does not certify meter or replace an independent omissions/additions review.

## Critical notes

- The book’s beauty depends on tonal adjacency: wounded men, divine cosmetics,
  seduction, sleep, storm, and battlefield blood. The final copyedit should let
  each register remain distinct without breaking the poem’s continuity.
- The Longfellow-inspired register should give Hera’s scene formal radiance while
  keeping its agency and danger visible; the later battle needs compressed force,
  especially in the stone and body passages.
- The meter report is only a syllable screen. A named human reader must still
  review stress, proper names, erotic and ceremonial diction, and the transitions
  between speech, simile, and combat.
