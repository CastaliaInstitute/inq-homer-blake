# Iliad Book 3 — read-aloud revision log

**Scope:** revised working passage, Iliad 3.1–244  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass  
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This log records a focused cadence pass on the opening 244 Greek lines. The
pass is deliberately limited: it does not certify the whole book, and it does
not substitute for a human reading aloud from the locked Greek alongside the
English.

## Changes made

- Re-lineated the crane and South-Wind similes into shorter, more audible
  blank-verse units while retaining their sequence and agents.
- Restored a more ceremonial procession in the Paris–Menelaus approach by
  giving the action its own beats: challenge, recognition, joy, and
  dismounting.
- Reduced prose-like coordination in Hector's rebuke and Paris's reply; kept
  the hostile compounds, the axe image, Aphrodite's gifts, and the proposed
  covenant.
- Reworked the truce speeches so the ritual conditions, elder counsel, lambs,
  and separate herald errands remain explicit.
