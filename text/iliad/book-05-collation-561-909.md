## Decisions recorded

- **561–627 — recovery and renewed battle:** Restored Menelaus and Antilochus beside
  the fallen, the deaths of Pylæmenes and Mydon, Hector’s advance with Ares, and
  Ajax’s failed attempt to strip Amphius.
- **628–698 — Tlepolemus and Sarpedon:** Preserved the Heracles–Laomedon genealogy,
  both taunts, the opposed wounds, the difficult extraction of Sarpedon’s spear,
  Odysseus’ choice, and the named Lycian deaths.
- **699–777 — Hector and the divine return:** Restored Hector’s pressure, the
  named Argive losses, Hera’s complaint, the golden chariot, Athena’s aegis and
  armor, and the landing at the Simoeis–Scamander confluence.
- **778–834 — Hera, Athena, and Diomedes:** Preserved the doves and lions/boars
  similes, Stentor’s rebuke, Athena’s recollection of Tydeus, Diomedes’ distinction
  between Aphrodite and Ares, and the charge against the war god.
- **835–909 — wounding and healing of Ares:** Restored Hades’ helmet, Periphas,
  Athena’s deflection, the guided spear beneath the belt, Ares’ cosmic complaint,
  Zeus’ rebuke, Paeon’s medicine, Hebe’s washing, and the goddesses’ return.
