- **274–296 — Pandarus falls:** Restored the spear exchange, Athena’s guidance beside the eye, the tongue and teeth wound, the loose horses, and the loss of Pandarus’s strength.

- **343–360 — Apollo and Ares:** Restored Apollo’s rescue of Aeneas, Diomedes’ taunt, and the correct handoff in which Apollo calls Ares to oppose Diomedes.
- **361–460 — Aphrodite and the gods:** Restored Iris and Ares’ horses, Dione’s examples of divine wounds, the healing of Aphrodite’s hand, Athena’s taunt, Zeus’s reply, Apollo’s threefold warning to Diomedes, Aeneas’s healing and image, and the commission to Ares.
