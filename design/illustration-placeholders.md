# Illustration placeholder deck

This is the working art brief for the forty-eight book slots in the two-volume
edition. Each entry is a **placeholder**, not a claim that an image exists or
has been approved for print. The source passage must be line-collated and
locked before a final plate is commissioned, generated, or selected.

## Production rules

- Trim: 6.625 × 10.25 in comic-size hardcover; full-bleed art requires 0.125 in bleed unless the
  printer template says otherwise.
- Target stock: 80# coated paper. Final files must be checked at placement
  size, in the printer's requested color profile, with embedded provenance.
- Art direction: visionary, severe, luminous, and symbolically legible;
  elongated gesture, rhythmic contour, compressed architectural space, and
  strong light/dark structure. This is a Blake-informed direction, not a copy
  of any Blake plate.
- Every original image must be credited as “Original plate by
  CastaliaInstitute; not by William Blake.” Historical material must retain
  artist roles, object record, scan source, rights statement, and caption.
- Do not render text, page borders, publisher marks, modern objects, fantasy
  armor, or anachronistic anatomy into the art. Keep a quiet outer margin for
  caption and trim safety.

## Placeholder records

Each record supplies the intended subject, a provisional source anchor, and a
prompt that can be handed to an illustrator or image-generation workflow.
“Exact range to lock” is deliberate: it prevents a compelling picture from
being treated as evidence of a passage that has not yet passed editorial
collation.

exact Greek/English line range, final caption, creator or model record,
provenance URL, rights status, dimensions, color profile, and final asset path.
Until then, the slot remains `placeholder / prompt-ready / not for print`.
