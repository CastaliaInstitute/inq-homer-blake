# Production brief

## Goal

Produce two cohesive illustrated volumes at a 7 × 10 inch trim size:

1. Homer's _Iliad_
2. Homer's _Odyssey_

The visual program should investigate Blake's Homeric and classical imagery,
using historical Blake material where a relevant public-domain work exists and
newly made plates to complete the visual sequence. New work must remain
explicitly distinguished from historical Blake material.

## Editorial scope

The project will preserve the complete twenty-four-book structure of each
epic. Each book should have a short editorial plan identifying key passages,
recurring figures, and opportunities for a plate, ornament, or opening image.

Each plate manifest record must include:

- epic and book, passage, or thematic transition;
- source type: `blake-original`, `historical-reference`, or `generated`;
- provenance and rights notes;
- caption and credit line;
- final file, dimensions, and color profile.

## Initial questions to resolve

- Which English translations will serve as the copy texts for each volume?
- Which Homeric subjects and classical references are represented in Blake's
  surviving work or relevant historical editions?
- What target number of plates best supports the pacing of forty-eight books?
- Will the two volumes share one type, ornament, and caption system?
- What model/version and prompt record will be retained for generated plates?

## Lulu production baseline

The current target is Lulu's 7 × 10 in trim with 80# White Coated interior
paper and Premium Color printing. A full-bleed interior page is 7.25 × 10.25
in, with 0.125 in bleed on each side. Final binding, page count, cover
spread, and spine width must be locked in Lulu before cover export. Each
volume will require one interior PDF and one cover PDF.

The authoritative preflight checklist is in `preflight/checklist.md`.

## Suggested milestones

1. Lock copy texts and source-image inventory.
2. Build a book-by-book image map for both epics.
3. Establish the 7 × 10 design system and proof pages.
4. Generate and review supplemental plate candidates.
5. Assemble print PDFs and run preflight checks.
6. Conduct literary, art-historical, and production QA.
