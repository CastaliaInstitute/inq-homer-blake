## Prompt

Create a high-resolution portrait illustration for Homer’s *Iliad*, Book 2,
showing the false dream and the mustering of the Achaean host as one original
concept plate for CastaliaInstitute. In the upper register, show sleeping
Agamemnon within a severe dark tent, receiving a luminous but ambiguous dream-
figure shaped as a messenger from Zeus; in the lower register, let the waking
host gather beside the ships in ordered ranks, banners and spear-lines becoming
calligraphic constellations. Keep the dream and the human assembly visibly
distinct but linked by a descending vertical beam of prophetic light. Visionary,
severe, luminous, symbolically legible art direction: elongated gesture,
rhythmic etched contour, compressed architectural space, strong light/dark
structure, original black ink with restrained bone-white, smoke-gray, muted
indigo, and antique-gold wash. Historical atmosphere without archaeological
clutter. Quiet outer margin for caption and trim safety; no text, border,
publisher mark, watermark, signature, modern objects, fantasy armor, gore, or
anachronistic anatomy. Blake-informed visual energy only; do not imitate,
reconstruct, or quote any specific William Blake plate.
