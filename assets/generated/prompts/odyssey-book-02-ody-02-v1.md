## Prompt

Create a high-resolution portrait illustration for Homer’s *Odyssey*, Book 2,
showing Telemachus’ assembly and secret departure as one original concept plate
for CastaliaInstitute. Compose three clear planes: in the foreground,
Telemachus stands upright before the assembly with the inherited scepter and a

black ink with restrained bone-white, smoke-gray, muted indigo, and antique-gold
wash. Make the prophetic road readable at 7 x 10 inches, with quiet outer
margin for caption and trim safety. No text, border, publisher mark, watermark,
signature, modern objects, fantasy armor, gore, or anachronistic anatomy.
Blake-informed visual energy only; do not imitate, reconstruct, or quote any
