# Longfellow translation standard

This repository's English Homer is new work translated from the locked Greek
copy text. Henry Wadsworth Longfellow's translation of Dante's *Divine Comedy*
is the governing formal model: flexible unrhymed iambic pentameter, patient
five-stress cadence, ceremonial diction, and a grave narrative flow.

## Non-negotiable source standard

- Every Greek line or source unit must map to an English line or an explicitly
  logged span of lines.
- No epithet, speech, simile, catalogue item, ritual act, kinship, or narrative
  event may disappear merely to regularize meter.
- Additions are permitted only for necessary English syntax and must be logged;
  explanatory additions belong in notes.
- Proper names, divine titles, repeated formulas, and governing terms must be
  consistent with `text/glossary.md`, with exceptions recorded.

## Verse standard

- Aim for natural five-stress lines with a flexible iambic movement; do not
  force every line into mechanical regularity.
- Read every completed passage aloud and log awkward stress, syntactic drag,
  and excessive enjambment.
- Each book's meter report must show at least 85% naturally five-stress lines;
  all outliers must be intentional, intelligible, and logged.
- The model governs cadence, dignity, and movement—not Longfellow's wording,
  Dante's imagery, or any inherited English translation.

## Approval evidence

Before a book advances beyond draft, its record must contain a source map,
omissions/additions report, Greek-fidelity review, meter report, read-aloud
revision notes, glossary/concordance check, and signed editorial decision log.
