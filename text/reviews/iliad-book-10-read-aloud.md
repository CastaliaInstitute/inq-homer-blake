# Iliad Book 10 — read-aloud and fidelity revision log

**Scope:** Iliad 10.1–579, current working translation  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass  
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This pass tests the night council, the volunteer mission, Dolon’s interrogation,
the Thracian camp, and the return with Rhesus’s horses. The book’s violence and
disguise require unusually exact sequence control. This log does not certify
meter or replace an independent omissions/additions review.

## Critical notes

- The book’s beauty lies in controlled nocturnal attention: ears before eyes,
  animal movement before heroic display, and intelligence before violence. Keep
  the diction dark without making the actors indistinguishable.
- The Longfellow-inspired register should not ennoble the raid into pageantry.
  Its grave music is most effective when the short tactical clauses retain fear,
  calculation, and consequence.
- The meter report is only a syllable screen. A named human reader must still
  review stress, proper names, repeated horse epithets, and the breath of the
  long interrogation.

## Source-fidelity correction recorded in this pass

exchange; the retained passage preserves the complete reward, volunteer, and
companion-selection sequence. This remains working evidence and requires
independent human confirmation before any gate can pass.
