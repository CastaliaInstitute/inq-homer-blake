# Iliad Book 6 — read-aloud revision log

**Scope:** Iliad 6.1–529, current working translation
**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This pass checks the battlefield catalogue, the Glaucus–Diomedes guest-friend
exchange, the Trojan prayer, and Hector's domestic scenes for narrative pacing,
ceremonial register, and fidelity-sensitive compression. It does not certify
meter or Greek accuracy. Independent source comparison, full stress analysis,
and human sign-off remain open.

## Critical notes

- The book's deepest formal movement is from anonymous battle to named lineage,
  then from lineage to household. The elevated style works best when each turn
  remains audible.
- The Bellerophon episode is especially vulnerable to agency errors. The
  corrected wording keeps Proetus as the sender of the deadly tablet and should
  receive a second-reader check against the locked Greek.
- The machine meter report cannot judge whether genealogical catalogues remain
  alive in the mouth. Human read-aloud review should test names, compounds, and
  speech transitions at the comic-size two-column measure.
