## Decisions recorded

- **1–36 — opening battle:** Restored Ajax’s strike on Acamas, Diomedes’ killing of
  Axylus and Calesius, the twin sons of Bucolion, and the named battlefield deaths.
- **37–65 — Adrastus:** Preserved the broken chariot, Adrastus’ ransom speech,
  Agamemnon’s hard interruption, and the exact order of the killing.
- **66–72 — Nestor:** Retained the distinction between taking living men and stripping
  the dead, which drives the following tactical turn.
- **73–118 — Helenus and Hector:** Restored Helenus’ command to Hector and Aeneas,
  the twelve heifers and robe promised to Athena, the comparison with Achilles,
  Hector’s return to the ranks, and the shield-rim image at his departure.
