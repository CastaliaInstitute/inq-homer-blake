# *Iliad*, Book 6, lines 119–311 — collation record

**Source lock:** `data/tlg0012/tlg001/tlg0012.tlg001.perseus-grc2.xml`

**Source revision:** PerseusDL `canonical-greekLit` commit `ac0bc60033f1f83990a5cf7f1e7fc2e0423e381a`

**Method:** The English was checked against the locked Greek XML by numbered line
range. The Diomedes–Glaucus encounter, the Lycurgus exemplum, Bellerophon’s full
genealogy, the guest-gift exchange, Hector’s arrival at Priam’s house, and the
temple procession were checked in sequence.

## Decisions recorded

- **119–143 — Diomedes’ challenge:** Preserved his distinction between mortal and
  immortal opponents and the Lycurgus–Dionysus warning.
- **144–211 — Glaucus’ genealogy:** Restored the leaves simile, Ephyre and Sisyphus,
  Anteia’s false charge, the folded death-tablet, the Chimera, Solymi, Amazons,
  Lycian ambush, royal marriage, Aleian wandering, and the deaths of Isander and
  Laodameia.
- **212–236 — guest-friendship:** Preserved Oeneus’ twenty-day welcome, the purple
  belt and golden cup, the mutual renunciation of spears, and Zeus’ ironic removal
  of Glaucus’ understanding.
- **237–311 — Hector in Troy:** Restored the Scaean gates, Priam’s fifty sons’ and
  twelve daughters’ chambers, Hecuba’s wine offer and refusal, the Sidonian robe,
  Theano’s priesthood, the twelve heifers, and Athena’s refusal.
