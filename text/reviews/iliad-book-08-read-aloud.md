# Iliad Book 8 — read-aloud and fidelity revision log

**Scope:** Iliad 8.1–565, current working translation  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** editorial working evidence; not a gate pass  
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted draft review; human sign-off required

This pass tests Zeus’s prohibition, the scales of fate, the Nestor rescue,
Teucer’s aristeia and wounding, the recall of Hera and Athena, and Hector’s
night assembly for audible momentum and fidelity-sensitive compression. It does
not certify meter or replace an independent omissions/additions review.

## Findings and actions

| Passage | Finding | Action or decision |
|---|---|---|
| 8.1–52 | Zeus’s council speech needs a monumental opening without losing its threat’s concrete geography. | Preserve Tartarus, the golden chain, the chariot to Ida, and Gargarus’s grove and altar; keep the threat intelligible when read aloud. |
| 8.53–176 | The battle turns through the scales, thunder, and Nestor’s trapped horses rather than through a simple tactical defeat. | Retain the two fates, the sinking Achaean day, Paris’s arrow, the tangled traces, and Diomedes’ rescue call. |
| 8.177–286 | Hector’s confidence, Agamemnon’s prayer, and Teucer’s first success form a clean counter-rhythm. | Preserve the four horse names, Andromache’s remembered care, the eagle with the fawn, the nine-man advance, and all eight named kills. |
| 8.287–390 | Teucer’s broken bow and Ajax’s shield create the book’s sharpest physical reversal. | Keep Archeptolemus, Kebriones, the collarbone wound, the numb hand, the child-under-mother simile, and the trench crossing; test proper names again in independent review. |
| 8.391–482 | The Hera–Athena sequence broadens battlefield frustration into divine memory and forecast. | Preserve Heracles’ labor, Thetis’s appeal, the Hours, Iris’s recall, and the prophecy tying Hector’s limit to Patroclus and Achilles. |
| 8.483–565 | Hector’s night orders deliberately turn victory into anxious preparation. | Preserve the river-side assembly, food and horse orders, fires, watch, rejected sacrifice, thousand fires, fifty men by each, and the final waiting horses. |

## Critical notes

- Beauty is strongest in the alternation between cosmic scale and tactile labor:
  the golden chain gives way to tangled reins, broken bowstring, trench stakes,
  fodder, and firewood. The final copyedit should protect those material nouns.
- The Longfellow-inspired register works when the repeated formulas carry gravity;
  the duel and Teucer’s wound need shorter clauses so the action can strike through
  the ceremonial diction.
- The meter report is only a syllable screen. A named human reader must still
  review stress, proper names, repeated epithets, and intentional outliers.
