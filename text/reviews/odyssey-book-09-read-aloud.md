# Odyssey Book 9 — read-aloud and fidelity review

**Review status:** translator self-review; independent reader still required

## Findings

The opening now keeps the formal singer-frame before Odysseus names himself and
restores his brief reference to both Calypso and Circe. The Cicones episode now
retains the comrades’ threefold cry before departure, a ritual detail that makes
the cost of the raid audible rather than merely numerical.

The review preserves the book’s major causal chain: refusal to leave Ismarus,
the storm and nine-day drift, the Lotus-eaters’ erasure of home, the Cyclopes’
lawless isolation, the concealed ship’s location, the wine and “Nobody” ruse,
the olive stake, the ram escape, the fatal boast, and Poseidon’s curse.

## Beauty and cadence

The long declarative movement suits Odysseus’s retrospective authority, while
the short speech exchanges around Polyphemus sharpen the danger. The repeated
“Nobody” should remain plain and emphatic; its comic surface must carry the later
tragic consequence. Read aloud, the ram passage needs a breath check so that the
three-by-three binding and the final ram’s pause remain visually intelligible.

## Open decisions

- Compare all numbers, named places, and sacrifice language against the full Greek.
- Check the Cyclops speeches for omitted epithets and the exact order of the waves.
- Commission an independent read-aloud and omissions/additions report.

**Conclusion:** key framing and mourning details restored; not approved for layout
or final publication.
